# EventFlow AI

## AI-Powered Mega-Event Hospitality & Orchestration Platform

> **Monitor → Predict → Explain → Recommend → Simulate → Apply → Guide → Updated State**

EventFlow AI is an AI-powered decision-support platform for managing complex mega-events.

It combines **event monitoring, crowd prediction, risk analysis, explainability, intervention recommendations, what-if simulation, and guided decision-making** into one platform.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Mega-events involve interconnected systems such as:

- Crowd movement
- Venue capacity
- Transportation
- Accommodation
- Infrastructure
- Operational resources

A change in one system can create pressure in another.

```text
Crowd Increase
      ↓
Venue Pressure
      ↓
Transport Congestion
      ↓
Delayed Movement
      ↓
Higher Operational Risk
